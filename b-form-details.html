    Versatile Modular Formwork

    The FORPRO B-form is our flagship modular wall formwork system. Engineered for rapid on-site assembly, it combines lightweight aluminum components with heavy-duty steel frames to deliver exceptional strength and perfect concrete finishes. Whether you are working on residential complexes, commercial towers, or heavy infrastructure projects, B-form adapts to your needs with unparalleled speed and reliability.

    Key System Advantages

    Rapid Assembly

    Quick-lock components reduce installation time by up to 40%.

    Reusable Panels

    High-grade materials ensure hundreds of repetitive uses without deformation.

    Superior Finish

    Precision-engineered smooth panels guarantee a flawless concrete surface.
