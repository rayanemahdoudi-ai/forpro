Automatic hydraulic climbing system — crane-free, one-worker operation, budget-saving, and time-efficient.

Self-climbing System Introduction

The SCF40 climbing formwork system that realizes automatic climbing through hydraulic devices has become the most popular product of our company.

The SCF40 system does not require a crane to climb and lower the platform. The climbing of multiple platforms only requires one worker to operate, which not only greatly reduces the workload of workers, but also greatly saves the budget and ensures the safety of the site.

In addition, compared with F16 and other systems that require a crane to lift, the use of SCF40 is also more time-saving.

Key System Advantages

100% Crane-Free

Hydraulic self-climbing mechanism completely removes the dependency on tower cranes.

One-Worker Operation

Advanced automation allows a single worker to safely manage multiple climbing platforms.

Highly Cost-Efficient

Greatly reduces labor needs and speeds up floor-to-floor transitions.
