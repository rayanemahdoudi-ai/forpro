Core System Components & Advantages

Designed with only three main components to ensure maximum customization and structural integrity.

Plywood

The Panels are designed and assembled according to customers of the project. The exposed concrete effect on the surface will be created.

Timber beam

We are able to customise the length of the Timber Beam according to the needs of the customers. They can be connected and extended depending on the requirements of the projects.

Steel waling

The steel waling has been tested to withstand a bending resistance of 11.54 kNm and shear limit of 84.81 kNm.
