Slab Formwork

Slab Formwork Introduction

FORPRO Slab Formwork System provides a safe, efficient and economical solution for concrete slab construction. The system is designed for fast installation, high load capacity, excellent concrete finishing, and is built for repeated use across multiple projects.

System Visuals

Key Components & Assembly

Explore the core physical structure of our slab system, designed for strength, speed, and easy installation on your job site.

Timber Beam & Steel Waling

Durable and flexible structural components that provide exceptional load capacity and precise alignment for flawless concrete slab construction.

T-form

Engineered for rapid deployment and stripping. This system saves significant time on large-scale projects by allowing large areas to be poured at once without repositioning individual props.
