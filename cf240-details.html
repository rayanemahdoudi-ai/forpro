    CF240 Climbing Formwork

    Heavy-duty crane-lifted climbing formwork system ideal for bridge pylons, dam walls, and high-rise towers up to 240m.

    Home > Products > CF240 Details

    Intelligent Modular System

    CF240 is an intelligent modular system. Most complex structural shapes can be constructed easily and flexibly.

    For ease of use, CF240 owns a Light-weight platform. What's more, we recommend that the CF240 climbing formwork system should be hoisted as a whole to meet the design requirements.

    On structures with vertical walls, CF240 permits pouring sections up to 6000mm high.

    Comparing with the F16 climbing formwork system, the CF240 climbing formwork system is much wider. It is 2400mm in width for the operating platform (also known as the middle platform), which provides plenty of space for working quickly and conveniently. Moreover, there are some similarities between the F16 climbing formwork system and the CF240 climbing formwork system. For instance, they both need a crane to complete the lift movement.

    Key System Advantages

    Intelligent Modularity

    Flexible system designed to easily adapt to the most complex structural shapes.

    High Reach Capability

    Engineered to allow pouring of vertical sections up to 6000mm high in a single pass.

    Spacious Platform

    Offers a 2400mm wide operating platform, providing plenty of room for safe and efficient work.
